Philanthropy

In Service to Athens Firefighters

Each semester, Alpha Mu's philanthropy events raise thousands of dollars for the Athens-Clarke County Fire Department, helping firefighters recover from burn injuries and keeping them equipped with up-to-date, life-saving equipment.

Our annual spring philanthropy week includes a volleyball tournament, percentage nights, a spring tailgate, and a powderpuff flag football tournament.

Dedicated to the memory of our brother, Conner Strickland.
