A Message from the President

A Chapter Growing in Size, Standing, and Purpose

This fall, Alpha Mu initiated one of its largest pledge classes in over a decade, welcoming 42 new members. We are among the largest chapters on campus, and our chapter GPA stands above the all-men's average and within the top ten of the Interfraternity Council.

Our brothers hold leadership positions in many of the University's most respected organizations and go on to careers from Los Angeles to New York at firms including Goldman Sachs, J.P. Morgan, Citi, Blackstone, and TNT Sports.

One of the First Pi Kappa Alpha Chapters in the Deep South

Chartered more than a century ago, Alpha Mu is among the oldest fraternities at the University of Georgia. Today the chapter resides in Greek Park, steps from Sanford Stadium.
