Since 1908

One of the Oldest Fraternities at Georgia

Alpha Mu was chartered in 1908, making it one of the oldest fraternities at the University of Georgia and one of the first Pi Kappa Alpha chapters in the Deep South.

Pi Kappa Alpha was founded at the University of Virginia in 1868 and has grown into one of the largest national fraternities, with more than 200 active chapters.

In 2009, the chapter moved from 360 South Lumpkin Street to a new house in Greek Park, just steps from Sanford Stadium.

From the President

Where Alpha Mu Stands Today

This fall we initiated one of our largest pledge classes in over a decade, with 42 new members. We are one of the largest chapters on campus and continue to grow in both size and campus involvement.

We proudly host the largest tailgates on campus, 500 feet from the base of Sanford Stadium, and our brothers go on to careers at firms including Goldman Sachs, J.P. Morgan, Citi, Blackstone, and TNT Sports.
